IMPORTANT UI REQUIREMENT:

The entire application MUST look like a real, professionally designed Government of India digital portal.

It must NOT look like a Vibe-Coded AI SaaS dashboard, startup landing page, Dribbble concept, or futuristic AI product.

VISUAL REFERENCE:
Take inspiration from professional Indian government digital platforms such as:

- Government of India portals
- Digital India ecosystem
- NIC-designed applications
- MoSPI / Ministry portals
- myScheme / India.gov.in style information architecture
- Government administrative dashboards

The design should feel like a system that could realistically be deployed by a Government Ministry.

CORE VISUAL PRINCIPLES:

1. GOVERNMENT-FIRST DESIGN

- Use a formal and institutional visual language.
- Prioritize clarity, accessibility, information density and usability.
- Avoid excessive decorative elements.
- Avoid trendy startup UI patterns.
- Avoid excessive rounded cards.
- Avoid unnecessary gradients.
- Avoid neon colors.
- Avoid glassmorphism.
- Avoid glowing effects.
- Avoid 3D illustrations.
- Avoid giant AI robot graphics.
- Avoid excessive animations.

2. COLOR SYSTEM

Use a restrained Government-style palette:

Primary:

- Deep Government Navy Blue
- Dark Blue
- White

Secondary:

- Muted Green
- Light Grey
- Medium Grey

Status colors:

- Green = Low Risk / Success
- Amber = Medium Risk / Warning
- Orange = High Risk
- Red = Critical Risk / Action Required

Use colors sparingly.

The majority of the interface should be white, light grey and dark blue.

3. HEADER

Create a formal Government-style header.

Left:

- Government of India emblem placeholder
- “Government of India”
- “Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation”
- Product name: “MP-Guard AI”

Right:

- Accessibility icon
- Language selector: English | हिंदी
- Help
- Notifications
- User profile

Do NOT use a startup-style logo-heavy header.

4. NAVIGATION

Use a conventional administrative sidebar.

Navigation should be compact and information-oriented:

Dashboard
Projects
AI Risk Monitoring
Alerts & Investigations
Financial Monitoring
Geo-Spatial Monitoring
Vendors & Beneficiaries
Reports
Audit Trail
Notifications
Administration

Use simple professional line icons.

The sidebar should NOT dominate the screen.

5. DASHBOARD LAYOUT

Use an information-dense government dashboard.

Top:
Page title + breadcrumb.

Example:

Home / Dashboard

“MP-Guard AI — MPLAD Monitoring Dashboard”

Below:
KPI summary cards.

Cards should be rectangular with subtle borders and minimal shadows.

Example:

TOTAL PROJECTS
1,284

FUNDS ALLOCATED
₹482.6 Cr

FUNDS UTILIZED
₹391.4 Cr

PROJECTS DELAYED
117

HIGH-RISK PROJECTS
43

Do not use oversized typography.

6. TABLES ARE IMPORTANT

Government applications rely heavily on data tables.

Use professional tables instead of excessive cards.

Example:

Project ID | Project Name | District | Approved Amount | Utilization | Progress | Risk | Status | Action

Use:

- Compact rows
- Clear column headers
- Pagination
- Sort icons
- Filters
- Search
- Export button

Tables should look like real administrative software.

7. FORM DESIGN

Use traditional professional government form patterns.

Inputs should have:

- Clear labels above fields
- Required indicator
- Helper text where required
- Simple rectangular borders
- Accessible focus state

Avoid floating labels and unnecessarily stylized inputs.

8. BUTTONS

Use simple rectangular buttons with subtle corner radius.

Primary:
“Search”
“Apply Filter”
“Generate Report”
“View Details”

Secondary:
“Reset”
“Export”
“Cancel”

Danger:
“Escalate”

Avoid pill-shaped buttons everywhere.

9. BREADCRUMBS

Every internal page should contain breadcrumbs.

Example:

Home / Projects / Project Details / MPLAD-2026-00482

10. PAGE TITLES

Use formal government terminology.

Instead of:
“AI Magic Dashboard”

Use:
“AI-Based Risk Monitoring”

Instead of:
“Fraud Radar”

Use:
“Fraud & Anomaly Detection”

Instead of:
“Smart Insights”

Use:
“Analytical Insights”

Instead of:
“AI Found Something”

Use:
“AI-Generated Risk Alert”

11. AI DESIGN

AI should be integrated professionally into the administrative workflow.

Do NOT put a giant AI robot illustration on the dashboard.

Instead use a small label:

AI ANALYSIS

Risk Score: 82/100
Confidence: 94%

Then show:

Detected Indicators
• Unusual expenditure pattern
• Project progress variance
• Vendor payment anomaly

Evidence
[View supporting data]

Recommended Action
Request financial verification

AI should assist government officers rather than appear as an autonomous futuristic system.

12. ALERT DESIGN

Use a formal alert table.

Severity | Alert ID | Project | Issue | Confidence | Date | Status | Action

Critical alerts should have a small red severity indicator, not a giant red card.

13. MAP DESIGN

Use a clean administrative GIS interface.

Map:

- Light neutral map
- State/district boundaries
- Small risk markers
- Simple legend

Avoid futuristic glowing maps.

14. CHARTS

Charts should resemble government analytical reports.

Use:

- Bar charts
- Line charts
- Pie/donut charts only where useful
- Tables
- Heatmaps
- Trend indicators

Keep chart colors restrained.

Every chart must have:

- Title
- Date/period
- Legend
- Source
- Filter option where relevant

Example:

“District-wise MPLAD Fund Utilization”
Financial Year: 2025–26
Source: MPLAD Project Records

15. OFFICIAL DATA LANGUAGE

Use formal terminology throughout.

Examples:

“Financial Year”
“Project Status”
“Fund Utilization”
“Implementing Agency”
“Administrative Approval”
“Physical Progress”
“Financial Progress”
“Project Sanction”
“Expenditure”
“Inspection Status”
“Risk Assessment”
“Verification Required”

Avoid casual startup language.

16. ACCESSIBILITY

The application should follow government accessibility principles.

Include:

- High text contrast
- Keyboard-friendly controls
- Visible focus states
- Accessible icons
- Text labels alongside icons
- Hindi/English language switch
- Adjustable text-size control
- Screen-reader-friendly semantic structure

17. GOVERNMENT PORTAL DETAILS

Add subtle details that make the application feel authentic:

- “Last Updated: 27 August 2026”
- “Data refreshed at: 13:30 IST”
- “System Status: Operational”
- “© Government of India”
- “Content managed by Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation”
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Accessibility Statement
- Contact / Helpdesk

18. FOOTER

Use a formal government footer.

Include:

Government of India
Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation

Important Links
Accessibility
Privacy Policy
Terms of Use
Contact Us

“Content owned and maintained by Government of India.”

19. RESPONSIVENESS

Design:

- Desktop: 1440px
- Laptop: 1280px
- Tablet: 768px

The layout should remain usable at different screen sizes.

20. OVERALL IMPRESSION

When a hackathon jury opens the prototype, the first impression should be:

“This looks like a genuine Government of India administrative monitoring system.”

NOT:

“This looks like an AI startup dashboard.”

The UI should communicate:

OFFICIAL
TRUSTWORTHY
SECURE
DATA-DRIVEN
ACCESSIBLE
ADMINISTRATIVE
PROFESSIONAL

Use the attached Smart India Hackathon problem statement as the functional source, but create a completely polished Government-style digital product UI from it.

Prioritize realistic government workflow and usability over visual effects.
